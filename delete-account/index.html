Delete your Impostor Online account

Last updated: 24 November 2026

For Impostor Online, the game by SkullSocket Studios. You can delete your account in the game or by e-mail.

In the game

On every platform, guests included:

  1. Open Impostor Online.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Press Delete Account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted at once. This can't be undone.

By e-mail

Write to info@skullsocketstudios.com. Say which platform you play on and the name you play under; on Steam, include your Steam profile link. We may ask for what we need to confirm that the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account (its player id and its sign-in links) and everything saved with it:

  • your coins
  • your items, bought ones included
  • your statistics and level
  • the players you blocked
  • your report count
  • the Steam id and name on your profile

What is kept

  • Results of past matches: a player id with a role, a seat, whether that player survived, and the outcome, with no name. Once your account is deleted, that id no longer leads to any account. A result is kept until its table plays its next match; if it plays no other match, with no set end.
  • Reports already posted to our moderation channel, until we delete them.
  • Purchase records that Steam (Valve) keeps under its own policy. A DLC you bought on Steam stays in your Steam library: it belongs to your Steam account, not to your game account.

After you delete

If you start the game again after deleting your account, it makes a new, empty account.

The Privacy Policy explains everything the game keeps.
