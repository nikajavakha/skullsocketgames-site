Impostor Online by SkullSocket Studios

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Impostor Online is made by SkullSocket Studios ("we", "us"). This page explains in plain words what the game keeps about you, where it is kept, why, for how long, and what you can do about it.

What we keep, and where

Your account

The first time you start the game, it makes an account for you in Unity Gaming Services, run by Unity Technologies. The account has a player id, linked to how you sign in:

  • On Steam: your Steam account id and your Steam name.
  • On Android: your Google Play Games player id and name, or a guest account. A guest's name is generated by the game and kept on your device.

What is saved with your account

These are saved with your account in Unity Cloud Save:

  • your coins
  • the cosmetic items you own, and the ones you have equipped
  • your match statistics and your level
  • the Steam order ids of your purchases
  • your Steam id and Steam name
  • the players you blocked (up to 100)
  • how many reports you sent today (you can send up to 10 a day)

Tables

While a table exists, its name, settings and language, and its players' names and player ids, are kept in Unity's lobby service.

When a match starts, the game's server keeps the player ids of the table's players. When the match ends, it keeps each player's id with their role, their seat, whether they survived, and the outcome. These records hold no names. They are kept until that table plays its next match; if it plays no other match, they have no set end.

Reports

During a match you can report a player from the Table (the list of players), choosing one of four reasons. The report is posted to our private moderation channel on Discord with the reason, both players' names and player ids, each player's Steam id where they have one, and the table's id. It is kept there until we delete it, and nowhere else.

Voice and chat

Voice is carried live by Vivox (part of Unity) between the players at your table. Text chat travels live between them through Unity's servers. We record and keep neither.

Names, table names and chat lines are checked against word lists on each player's own device, and listed words are masked.

"Tell Discord I'm waiting"

If you press Tell Discord I'm waiting, the game posts your table's player count, size and language to the game's public Discord server. No names are posted.

Payments

Payments are handled by Steam (Valve). We never see your card or payment details.

No ads, no tracking

The game has no ads, and no advertising or tracking. We sell no data.

Who processes data for us

  • Unity Technologies: Authentication (your account), Cloud Save (what is saved with it), Cloud Code (the game's server), Lobby (tables), Relay (connections between players) and Vivox (voice).
  • Valve: Steam sign-in and purchases.
  • Google: Google Play Games sign-in on Android.
  • Discord: our moderation channel, and the "Tell Discord I'm waiting" post.

These are US companies. Your data may be processed in the United States and in other countries.

Why we use it

  • To run the game: signing you in, running tables and saving your progress.
  • To process purchases.
  • To keep play safe: the word filter, reports, blocks and bans.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything saved with it: until you delete your account.
  • A match's player ids and results: until that table plays its next match; if it plays no other match, with no set end.
  • Reports: in our moderation channel until we delete them.
  • Purchases: Steam keeps its own records of them, under Valve's policy.

Your rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data.
  • You can delete your account in the game, in Settings → Account → Delete Account, or by e-mail. Delete your account has the steps, and what is deleted and what is kept.
  • You can object to how we use your data.
  • You can complain to the data-protection authority where you live.

To ask for any of these, write to info@skullsocketstudios.com.

Children

Impostor Online is for players 13 and up, and it is not directed at children under 13. If a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top says when it last changed.
